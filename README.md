## Project Overview

SoleDrop is a fictional sneaker and streetwear reatail store founded by a group of sneaker enthusiasts passionate about street culture and urban fasion.What began as a small pop-up store at a local markets has grown into a recognised brand within the snaeker community.SoleDrop specialises in limited edition sneakers,and streetwear for evryday fasion lovers.

## Website Goals and Objectives

-Create an online presence and reflects the brand's streetwear identity and culture.<br>
-Showcase available and upcoming sneaker drops.<br>
-Drive product enquiries and in-store visitors.<br>
-Build a loyal community of followers and repeat customers.
